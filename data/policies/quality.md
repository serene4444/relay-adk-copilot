# Linnet — Quality Promise

## 1. What is covered
Every Linnet item is guaranteed against defects in materials and workmanship for **1 year from the order date**. Defects include:
- Seams that come apart
- Broken zippers, buttons, or hardware
- Fabric flaws present at delivery

## 2. What is not covered
- Normal wear and tear, such as pilling or fading over time
- Shrinkage or damage from not following the care label
- Alterations made after purchase
- **One-of-a-kind vintage pieces**, which are sold as-is

## 3. How it works
Send a photo of the defect and the order number to customer care. Approved claims get a **free repair, replacement, or full refund**, at Linnet's choice. No return window applies to approved quality claims within the 1-year period.

## 4. Final sale items
Final sale items (see the Return Policy) **are still covered** by the Quality Promise if they have a genuine defect.
