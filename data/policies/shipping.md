# Linnet — Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping costs
- **Standard shipping is free** on orders of **$75 or more** (before tax).
- Orders under $75 pay a flat **$8** for standard shipping.
- **Express shipping** costs **$20** regardless of order total.

## 2. Delivery times
- Standard: **4 to 7 business days** after the order ships.
- Express: **2 business days** after the order ships.
- Orders placed before 12 p.m. Central Time on a business day ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day.

## 3. Where we ship
- We ship to all 50 U.S. states.
- Express shipping is **not available to P.O. boxes**. Standard shipping is.
- We do not currently ship internationally.

## 4. Order status codes
- **SHP**: shipped, on the way
- **DLV**: delivered
- **CXL**: cancelled, not shipped (no return needed; any charge is refunded automatically)

## 5. Return shipping
Return shipping is free for returns within the full refund window. Returns in the store credit window have a **$6** return label fee deducted from the credit.
