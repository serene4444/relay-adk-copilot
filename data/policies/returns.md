# Linnet — Return Policy

Effective January 1, 2026. Applies to all orders placed on linnet.example or in Linnet stores.

## 1. Full refund window
Most items can be returned within **30 days of the order date** for a full refund. Items must be unworn and unwashed, with original tags attached.

## 2. Store credit window
Items returned **31 to 45 days** after the order date are eligible for **store credit only**, under the same condition rules as Section 1. After 45 days, returns are not accepted.

## 3. Final sale items
The following items are **final sale** and cannot be returned or exchanged:
- Intimates (bralettes, bodysuits, underwear) and swimwear, for hygiene reasons
- Earrings, for hygiene reasons
- One-of-a-kind vintage pieces
- Any item marked FINAL SALE at checkout

## 4. Refund timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days** after the return is received at our warehouse.

## 5. Exchanges
Linnet does not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

## 6. Damaged or defective items
Items that arrive damaged or defective are covered by the Linnet Quality Promise, not this return policy, and can be reported at any time within one year.
